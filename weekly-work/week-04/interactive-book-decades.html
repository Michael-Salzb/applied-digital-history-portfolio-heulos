Recorded editions in the teaching sample, by decade

Choose your local book-sample.csv. This page counts edition records using the existing decade column. It does not measure total historical book production or readership.

1. Choose your CSV

Your file is processed entirely in this browser. No file contents are uploaded, and no internet connection or external libraries are needed.

2. Interactive bar chart

Each bar represents a decade. Its height and number above it show the count of recorded editions in this teaching sample. Hover over, click, or keyboard-focus a bar for details; press Enter or Space to select it. The numerical axis starts at zero. Scroll horizontally if needed.
