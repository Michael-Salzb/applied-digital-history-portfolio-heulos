### How could you use text analysis with Voyant in a research project? Briefly describe the corpus you might use, the question you would investigate, and one limitation you would need to consider.  

To be completely honest during the excercise I had a hard time thinking of use-cases for this tool apart from the very obvious "word-count" and "frequency" aspects.
One major issue with analysing historical texts with Voyant that immediately came to mind was the fact that language heavily changes over time, so any analysis would have to focus on a very specific time-frame/period. But this might not be justified since I have not tested, how the tool would behave if there are multiple occurrences of the same word written differently.
In my mind therefore one would have to use a rather uniform set of texts, otherwise some content may fall through. Regardless Voyant can definitely aid with questions regarding the quantifying of text and the comparison of texts.
If (and it is probably possible) one can get the Trend tab to show the results chronologically the research and analysis benefits heavily.
Questions like "How has the use of X changed over time? (quantity as well as quality)" or "How can spikes in relative/raw frequency be traced back to or linked to historical events?"

Overall, it might just be a "me-problem" so i'm very curious already about what the others in class have to say.
